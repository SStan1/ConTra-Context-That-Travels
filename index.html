Method

ConTra uses reinforcement learning (RL) to train Qwen3.5 models to write compact, source-supported context prefixes. At indexing time, the model reads the target chunk and its surroundings. Its prefix travels with the chunk through embedding retrieval and reranking, and is reused across queries.

RealContextBench

Five application collections naturally mix self-contained and context-dependent passages. Each collection has a shared retrieval corpus with targets mapped from human-annotated answers or evidence.
